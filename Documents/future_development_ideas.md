# Future Development Ideas

This document outlines potential features and improvements for future development of the Generative AI Playground:

- **Voting System for Models:** Allow users to rate or provide feedback on AI models.
- **Locally Run Models:** Introduce support for integrating and running models locally within the playground.
- **Video Generation Models:** Expand the capabilities to support video synthesis and generation AI models.
- **Live Webcam AI Analysis:** Connect a live webcam feed, enabling a vision-capable AI model to analyze and describe what it sees in real-time.
- **Statistics Page:** A dedicated dashboard for tracking usage metrics and model performance runtimes.
- **Cost Visibility:** Display the estimated costs of running models and prompts to the users executing them. This could be integrated into the new user-facing statistics view or directly in the prompt interface.
